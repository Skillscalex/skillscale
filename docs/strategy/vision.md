# Skillscale Vision

Skillscale is **global skills and outcome infrastructure**: a living graph of skills, AI agents, tools, people, organizations, trust, reputation, learning loops, solutions, and monetizable outcomes.

## Strategic thesis

The world needs infrastructure that maps capability to outcomes. Skillscale should become the layer where skills are discovered, composed, verified, automated, learned, monetized, and continuously improved by human and AI actors.

## Product pillars

1. **Living skills graph** — canonical graph of skills, dependencies, agents, tools, outcomes, and evidence.
2. **Trust and reputation** — reputation edges, verification signals, audit history, reviews, usage outcomes, and safety scoring.
3. **AI orchestration** — agents that research, design, build, audit, coach, and deliver outcomes through clear boundaries.
4. **Continuous learning** — feedback loops that turn usage, reviews, and outcomes into graph updates and recommendations.
5. **Flexible monetization** — subscriptions, usage fees, marketplace take rate, enterprise licensing, and value-share agreements.

## Non-goals

- Do not become a static list of skills.
- Do not ship production changes without human approval.
- Do not let agents self-approve implementation.
- Do not leak private user graph data or secrets.

## Success metrics

- Mirror coverage of canonical skills data.
- Search/discovery quality and latency.
- Verified outcomes completed through Skillscale.
- Trust/reputation signal density per skill and agent.
- Conversion to paid subscriptions/usage/value-share.
- Deployment lead time with zero skipped gates.
